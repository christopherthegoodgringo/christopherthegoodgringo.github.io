Porthos Steakhouse & Pub

Grilled steaks, drinks and a night out in Santa Marta.

Get directions Details
Our review

Worth it for a steak night

5/5
  • Food5/5
  • Service5/5
  • Value5/5
  • Atmosphere5/5

We went to Porthos for Valentine's Day dinner, and it hit the spot. The steak came out cooked exactly how we asked, the staff checked on us without hovering, and the place has a relaxed pub feel. If you are craving a proper steak in Santa Marta, this is an easy pick.

The good

  • Steak cooked exactly how we asked
  • Friendly staff who checked on us without hovering
  • Fair price for the quality

Could be better

  • It gets busy on weekend nights, so reserve ahead or go early

Paying

We paid by card, and they take cash too. The 10% service charge was already on the bill, and we left an extra 5% tip because the service was great.

Getting there

It's a short walk from Parque de los Novios. We took a taxi, and the staff can call one for you when you're ready to leave.
