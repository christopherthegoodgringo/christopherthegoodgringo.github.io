How it works

No cost to join, no experience needed. You work when you want, from anywhere.

1

Sign up free

Create your agent account below. You get your own personal link and code right away.

2

Talk to businesses

Pick businesses from your Prospecting list and send them the ready-made messages by WhatsApp or email, or add businesses you know.

3

Get paid

When a business you brought in signs up through your link and pays for Silver, Gold or Platinum, you earn 30% of its first month, then 15% every month it keeps paying.

Agent terms

  • You are an independent agent, not an employee. You choose your own hours and are responsible for your own taxes.
  • Be honest and friendly. Only promise what the listings really include, and never pressure a business.
  • No spam: contact each business a few times at most, and stop if they say no.
  • Write to businesses in Spanish, unless they ask for English.
  • A business belongs to the first agent who contacts it or whose link the owner uses. You earn 30% on its first paid month and 15% on each monthly renewal after that.
  • If a payment is refunded, the commission for it is cancelled.
  • Christopher The Good Gringo can turn off an agent account that breaks these terms.

Questions

Does it cost anything to join?

No. Signing up is free and there are no monthly fees.

Do I need experience?

No. Your Prospecting list has ready-made messages; you just send them and answer questions.

Where can I work from?

Anywhere. Most agents work from their phone with WhatsApp.

How do I get paid?

In “My earnings” you connect a free Stripe account with your bank details. Commissions are sent automatically 14 days after each payment.

How do businesses get credited to me?

Your personal link is already included in your Prospecting messages. When a business owner opens it and signs up, they’re credited to you.
