All businesses

Every business on the site. Pick a country and city to narrow the list. Edit details or the level directly (your changes go live right away), remove a business from the site, or remove its owner.

Prospecting

Free (Bronze) businesses that nobody has claimed yet. Save each one's WhatsApp or email, then tap Send WhatsApp or Send email: your message opens already written, with their name, city and links filled in. The list keeps track of who you have contacted. If a business doesn't reply, a follow-up is due 4 days later and a last one 7 days after that: pick Follow-up due now to see who's next, and the send buttons use the right message.

Automatic emails

All day, spread evenly every hour, businesses in this list that have an email get your first message, then the follow-ups when they're due, sent from your own mailbox (christopher@christopherthegoodgringo.com). When a business replies, it's marked Replied and gets no more follow-ups. Agents' leads are left to the agents.
